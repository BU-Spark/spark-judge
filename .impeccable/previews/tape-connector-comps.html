Three answers to one question: how does the live step on the tape connect to the event card it feeds? Same data, same geometry (live event at 55.3%), same 96px channel. Pick one and it merges into the base preview, then propagates to the Code & Tell and Demo Day variants in their mode colors.

A

The Dogleg

Routed like a PCB trace: 45° chamfered elbows, the horizontal leg runs in a milled bus gutter, and the jack is always at the same address — engraved 03 IN, third socket from the left, like a patch bay.

The Tape Loop

The most literal answer: the tape itself lifts off the reel at the live step, loops up in a slack arc, and feeds into a reader slot on the module — the display is reading this moment off the tape.

The Tonearm

A pivoted gauge arm mounted on the module's edge swings down and rests its needle on the live step — like a tonearm dropping onto the track that's playing. The arm's angle is the readout: it steepens as the semester advances.
